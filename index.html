What I do

Custom software and automation tools, built around a real workflow rather than a generic template.

01

Arkiv

Document intelligence pipeline — automated processing and archiving of incoming documents, with OCR and structured extraction built in.

  • Automated document intake
  • OCR & structured data extraction
  • Production-ready pipelines
02

Autorep (ETA Bot)

Automated ETA lookup for inbound email requests. Reads .msg/.txt emails, extracts shipment references, matches them against a booking workbook, and writes reply-ready JSON for downstream automation such as Power Automate.

03

Processes and analyzes pallet shipment data with — works across multiple Excel formats, generates professional reports, and handles different customer types.
